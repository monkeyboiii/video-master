### English voiceover

Have you ever wondered why a dirt bike can kick you back—when you’re the one kicking it?

It’s not the engine starting backward. It’s the crank losing a split-second tug-of-war just before top dead center.

Press the lever: the starter gears turn the crank, and the crank drives the piston upward, compressing the mixture.

A spark just before top dead center is normal—the flame needs time to build. With enough momentum, the crank crosses the top, pressure peaks after it, and the engine keeps turning forward.

But if pressure builds too soon, the crank pin is still on the approaching side. Combustion pushes the piston down. The connecting rod pushes that offset pin at an angle—and creates reverse torque.

The crank slows, stops, then reverses a few degrees. Because the starter gears are still engaged, that force travels back through the kickshaft and lever—straight into your boot.

That’s kickback: a brief reversal, then a stall—not an engine running backward.

Being in gear isn’t the main cause. If it keeps happening, check the ignition timing, decompressor, and starting technique.

### 中文旁白

你有没有想过——明明是你在踩启动杆，它为什么还能反过来踹你一脚？

这不是发动机倒着启动了，而是曲轴在上止点前，输掉了一场不到一秒的拔河。

你踩下启动杆，启动齿轮带动曲轴，曲轴再把活塞推上去，压缩混合气。

点火发生在上止点前，本来很正常——因为燃烧需要时间。只要曲轴靠惯性跨过最高点，压力峰值在上止点后到来，它就会继续正转。

但如果缸压来得太早，曲柄销还在“正在靠近”的那一侧。燃烧向下压活塞，连杆斜着推向偏心的曲柄销，于是产生反向扭矩。

曲轴先减速，再停住，然后倒转几度。启动齿轮还在咬合，这股力就沿原路返回：

曲轴、齿轮、启动轴、启动杆——最后踹回你的脚。

这就是回踢：短暂倒转，然后停机，并不是发动机持续反转。

挂挡不是主因。如果经常回踢，就要检查点火正时、自动减压机构和启动方法。
