# Brief — DBX-APP-S03E003.8 share-your-trails

The interstitial that steps out of the field mission to show what the mission produced:
**visitor trail upload, private by default.** A rider asked for it in a DM while the founder
was out cold-messaging track owners; this is the feature and the reason it exists, in 41
seconds (en-US) / 44 seconds (zh-CN).

## Viewer (one concrete person)

Someone who rides trails nobody has mapped and has never posted them anywhere — because
posting them has always meant giving them away. The thing standing between them and
contributing is not effort, it is **exposure**.

## Pain point, in the viewer's words

- en-US: "I'm not putting my spots on the internet." (They aren't refusing to share. They're
  refusing to broadcast, and every tool so far has made those the same thing.)
- zh-CN: "我那条线不想到处发。" A separately authored zh-CN read is recorded and cut
  (VO_ZH_007) — and it goes further than the en one: the rider who asked for the feature
  **says his own line on the soundtrack** instead of being played by the founder.

## One-sentence viewer benefit (the core conclusion this video proves)

You can put a trail on the map without making it public, and you can change your mind in
either direction with one tap — so sharing costs you nothing you can't take back.

## Topic formula (one line)

Pain (I won't broadcast my spots) + solution (private by default, one tap either way) +
benefit (contribute without giving anything away).

The structure is request-then-delivery: a user's DM sets the premise in the first ten
seconds, and the rest of the episode is the founder answering it on camera.

## Designed reactions (the judgment test — all four must have real answers)

- Like because: "Yeah, we're gatekeeping all the way" — saying the quiet part out loud about
  a feature most products would market as *openness*.
- Save because: **genuinely strong here, and the first time in S03.** This one is a how-to;
  a viewer who wants to upload later will save it. E001/E002/E003/E003.5 all scored this
  weak and the note said don't force it — this episode doesn't have to.
- Comment because: the gatekeeping line will draw both "finally" and "this is why the sport
  doesn't grow", and that argument is the engagement.
- Follow because: it shows the founder ships what people ask for, which is a different
  promise from the mission episodes' "he actually goes".

## Go / no-go question for the human

1. **Does the shipped flow match what's described?** "Upload your trail. It's private by
   default" and the one-tap public/private toggle are stated as facts about the product. If
   the shipped behaviour differs in any detail — a confirm step, a delay, a default — this
   episode becomes a promise the app breaks on first use.
2. **Is "gatekeeping" the right joke to lead the trust claim with?** It is the strongest line
   in the cut and it is also the one that can be quoted against the product. **ASSUMPTION:**
   the operator wants it; it was in the rough script verbatim.
3. **"track owners get their spot" is gone for the second take running.** Take 1 had it in the
   closing map beat; takes 2 and 3 go straight from "lives on the same map" to what riders
   get. As recorded, the episode no longer says anything to track owners — the audience E003
   spent a whole episode with, and half of what "one map, two kinds of pin" was supposed to
   mean. Deliberate?
4. **The ending changed from a plea to a question, in both variants.** zh closes on
   这是不是还算比较有用？ and en take 3's unreadable closer transcribes as "Do you think it's
   a good idea?" — the same move. That is a different episode ending: it hands the last beat
   to the comments instead of asking for the download. Confirm it is what you want.
5. **The DM rider is a second identifiable person on the zh soundtrack.** Nothing on file.
   Separate question from E003's barber.
6. Both locales exist. They are **siblings, not translations** — the zh read is 3.3s longer,
   spends more on say-less and send-the-link, and closes 2.4s shorter. Confirm the two are
   meant to diverge that much before the shoot locks a single storyboard for both.
