# Brief — DBX-APP-S03E003.5 barber-shop-dirt-bike

The interstitial between E003 and E004: the **same barber, the same track**, told as a
portrait instead of a search. A 53-year-old barber in Hangzhou has spent close to a million
yuan building a dirt bike track — a first drop like jumping off a three-storey building, a
20-metre table — and has trained enough apprentices that their kids now ride it too.
