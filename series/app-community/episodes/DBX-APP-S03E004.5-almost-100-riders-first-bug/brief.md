# Brief — DBX-APP-S03E004.5 almost-100-riders-first-bug

**An interstitial, not a track.** The channel's own housekeeping: nearly 100 riders in the
app, an airtime card for the first 66 comments, and then the founder meets one of those

## Viewer (one concrete person)

Someone who already downloaded the app — one of the ~100 — and opened it once. It came up in
a language they don't read, they assumed it was broken or not for them, and they haven't
opened it since. They have not written to anyone about it, because nobody writes to anyone
about a free app.

## Pain point, in the viewer's words

"I downloaded it, it was in Chinese, I closed it." The bug is trivial and the consequence is
not: a locale default is the difference between a user and an uninstall, and it is invisible
to the person who built it because it never once happened on his phone.

## One-sentence viewer benefit (the core conclusion this video proves)

The thing you thought was broken is a known bug with a fix you can do right now in profile
settings — and the person who built it will say so on camera rather than quietly patch it.

## Topic formula (one line)

Milestone (快 100 个车友) + reward (前 66 个评论) + apology (说声 sorry) + the story that
earns it (遇到了下载这个 App 的哥们儿) + the bug, owned (第一个 Bug 已经找到) + the fix
(个人偏好设置).

The order is the whole design: the giveaway is spent before the bad news, so the apology
reads as candour rather than as damage control. **ASSUMPTION** — the operator ordered it this
way deliberately; nothing in the takes says so.

## Designed reactions (the judgment test — all four must have real answers)

- **Like because:** he found out his own app was broken by bumping into a stranger at a track,
  and he tells that story against himself.
- **Save because:** **weak, and the beat that carries it is the-workaround.** "Profile settings,
  get rid of the yellow triangle" is saveable only if the screen is actually shown. If the edit
  narrates it instead of demonstrating it, this reaction has no answer at all.
- **Comment because:** the ask is explicit and capped at 66, which is the only comment driver
  in S03 so far that doesn't rely on the topic being argued about. Expect the count to be
  disputed in the comments ("是不是已经满了") — that is fine, it's engagement.
- **Follow because:** the promise here is different from the track episodes. Those promise he
  goes; this promises he ships, listens, and admits. **The bug being real is what makes this
  land — a manufactured one would be worse than none.**
