# Brief — DBX-APP-S03E002 ebike-park-day-two

Day two of the 100-track mission. Same format as S03E001, but the episode carries a real
argument underneath the comedy: **e-bikes count**, and the app is being built to include
them rather than gatekeep them.

## Viewer (one concrete person)

Two people at once, which is the point:
1. A dirt bike rider who is quietly skeptical that an e-bike park belongs in a dirt bike app.
2. An e-bike/e-MTB rider who assumes apps like this aren't for them and never bothered.

Both need to see the founder resolve it on camera.
