## Title variants (ranked; label each with its opening formula A/B/C/D)

1. "I went home to ride — and every bike had a battery" — formula: D counterintuitive — why: states the reversal in one line and forces the question the whole video answers (does that still count?).
2. "Track 2 of 100: the e-bike park" — formula: B identity contrast — why: carries the series counter and names the argument; best once S03 has an audience.
3. "Do e-bikes count as dirt bikes?" — formula: A pain point — why: the comment-bait framing; highest engagement, highest risk of a hostile thread.
4. "The park I grew up on went electric" — formula: C benefit promise — why: leads with nostalgia rather than the debate; softest, safest read.

## Cover concept

- Background image intent: founder in the foreground looking at a rack of e-bikes / a rider on an e-bike mid-jump behind him, hometown mountains visible. The battery must be readable at thumbnail size — it's the whole hook.
- On-cover text (≤2 lines, legible at thumbnail size): **"THEY ALL HAVE BATTERIES NOW"** — the reversal, stated flat.
- Aspect(s) needed per platform: tiktok / reels / ytshorts 9x16.
- Emotional read at a glance: someone coming home and finding it changed.
