# Brief — DBX-APP-S03E000 track-zero

Season 3's opener. Not an episode of the format — the thing that makes the format legible.
It states the problem (the scene is invisible), the mission (100 tracks), and the narrator
(a rookie, admittedly unqualified), then hands off to S03E001 which starts the count.

## Viewer (one concrete person)

Someone who rides, or is one video away from wanting to, and has never seen their local
scene represented anywhere. They will not sit through a mission statement from a brand —
but they will follow a person who admits he isn't qualified and is going anyway.

## Pain point, in the viewer's words

- en-US: "Nobody outside this knows it exists — there's no reason anyone would ever find us."
- zh-CN: "在他们的印象里，没这种东西的。" — the Chinese hook is the pain point, said by the founder.

## One-sentence viewer benefit (the core conclusion this video proves)

Someone is finally going to go and document this scene in person, one track at a time, and
you can watch it from episode zero.

## Five-dimension scores (1–5, with one line of justification each)

| Dimension | Score | Why |
|-----------|------:|-----|
| User relevance 用户相关 | 4 | Speaks to the whole scene rather than a specific riding problem; broad but real. |
| Emotional trigger 情绪触发 | 5 | Invisibility, then someone volunteering to fix it, then admitting he's a rookie. |
| Information density 信息密度 | 2 | Deliberately low — this is a premise video. **Risk: at ~32s it must not feel like a trailer for nothing. The 100-track number is the only hard fact and has to land.** |
| Personal positioning 人设定位 | 5 | "Just a rookie rider with a big heart" is the series' whole persona in one line. |
| Interaction conversion 互动转化 | 4 | Follows, not comments — the count is what people subscribe to. Track nominations start in E001. |

## Topic formula (one line)

Pain (the scene is invisible) + solution (one rookie visiting 100 tracks in person) +
benefit (you get to watch it exist, from zero).

## Designed reactions (the judgment test — all four must have real answers)

- Like because: "Am I remotely qualified? Absolutely not." — the honesty is the hook.
- Save because: weak, and correctly so for a premise video. Don't force it.
- Comment because: lighter than the rest of S03 by design; track nominations belong to E001
  onward. If a comment prompt is wanted here, it is "where should track one be?"
- Follow because: the count starts at 0 / 100 on screen. That is the subscribe argument, and
  it is the only thing this episode is really selling.
