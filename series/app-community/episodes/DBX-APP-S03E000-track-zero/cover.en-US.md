## Title variants (ranked; label each with its opening formula A/B/C/D)

1. "I'm visiting 100 dirt bike tracks. Am I qualified? Absolutely not." — formula: D counterintuitive — why: pairs the scale of the mission with the admission that undercuts it; the contradiction is the whole appeal.
2. "To the rest of the world, this scene doesn't exist" — formula: A pain point — why: the hook line verbatim; lands hardest with people already inside the scene.
3. "Track Zero" — formula: B identity contrast — why: works only once the series has an audience, but it's the cleanest series-opener framing.
4. "Nobody's ever documented this. So I'm doing all 100." — formula: C benefit promise — why: leads with the payoff rather than the self-deprecation.

## Cover concept

- Background image intent: founder alone at an empty or unremarkable track — the shot must read as *nowhere in particular*, which is the hook's whole argument. Flat daylight, no hero grade.
- On-cover text (≤2 lines, legible at thumbnail size): **"TRACK 0 / 100"** with **"am I qualified? no"** small beneath — the counter plus the disclaimer.
- Aspect(s) needed per platform: tiktok / reels / ytshorts 9x16. Add rednote 3x4 if the Chinese-hook cut ships there.
- Emotional read at a glance: someone standing at the start of something far too big for them.
