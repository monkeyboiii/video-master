# Brief — DBX-APP-S03E001 100-tracks-day-one

Season 3 opener. New format for the series: a **numbered field mission** — visit 100 real
dirt bike tracks, onboard the owner and the riders at each one, count it on screen. This
episode establishes the format and the running gag; every later episode inherits both.

## Viewer (one concrete person)

A rider who scrolls past app ads without seeing them, but will watch a person actually walk
up to a track owner and get told yes or no. They like watching someone attempt an absurd,
concrete goal — and they want to know whether their own local track is on the list.

## Pain point, in the viewer's words

- en-US: "Every riding app is empty where I ride — nobody's actually gone out and signed up my track."
- zh-CN: deferred (en-US first; S03 is shot in English).

## One-sentence viewer benefit (the core conclusion this video proves)

The community isn't being announced — it's being built in person, one real track at a time,
and you can watch it happen.

## Five-dimension scores (1–5, with one line of justification each)

| Dimension | Score | Why |

## Topic formula (one line)

Pain (no app has my track) + solution (he's literally visiting 100 of them) + benefit
(yours could be next, and you can watch it get added).

## Designed reactions (the judgment test — all four must have real answers)

- Like because: the escalation gag (bow / beg / tear up) and the track dog.
- Save because: weak — not a goal for a format pilot; don't fake a checklist.
- Comment because: "come to my track" / naming their local spot. This is the primary
  conversion and the series' whole engine — the comments become the visit list.
- Follow because: the counter (1 / 100) promises 99 more; the ending designs that.

seasons, or a bit? If it's a bit, the ending needs to stop implying a literal count.
