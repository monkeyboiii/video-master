## Title variants (ranked; label each with its opening formula A/B/C/D)

1. "I'm visiting 100 dirt bike tracks to build a community" — formula: D counterintuitive — why: the number is the hook; it states an absurd, concrete, checkable commitment and opens the loop the counter pays off.
2. "Track 1 of 100" — formula: B identity contrast — why: reads as an ongoing series from the first frame; strongest if the account already has traction.
3. "How to get a whole dirt bike track to join your app" — formula: C benefit promise — why: reframes the comedy as a method video; useful A/B against the mission framing.
4. "Nobody's app has my local track. So I drove to one." — formula: A pain point — why: leads with the viewer's frustration instead of the founder's goal.

## Cover concept

- Background image intent: founder mid-stride walking toward real riders at the track, bikes and people clearly in frame — it must be obvious this is a real place, not a studio. Natural daylight, no grade that looks like an ad.
- On-cover text (≤2 lines, legible at thumbnail size): **"TRACK 1 / 100"** with **"one down"** small underneath — the counter *is* the packaging.
- Aspect(s) needed per platform: tiktok / reels / ytshorts 9x16.
- Emotional read at a glance: an actual person doing an unreasonable thing in a real place.

## First frame (0.0s)

Founder already talking and already walking — mid-motion, track visible behind. The "100"
begins to pop on screen within the first beat. No logo, no title card, no static hold.
