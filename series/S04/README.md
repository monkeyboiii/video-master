# S04 — Product & feature introductions

What the field mission produced, shown as product. Each episode steps out of the 100-track run to
introduce one thing the app gained, then goes back. They were made as interstitials inside S03 with
one-decimal ids; a fraction said *when* a piece published and could never say *what it was*, so
they were renumbered into a season that can be counted and reported on.
