# Brief — S04E001 put-it-on-the-map

The interstitial briefed as **S03E002.5**: it publishes between E002 and E003 and does not
advance the 100-track counter. One rider sent a GPX of his local trail and asked for it to
be findable; this episode is the answer, and the ask it makes of everyone else.

## Viewer (one concrete person)

A trail rider who has a GPX on his phone — from his own rides, or from the one guy in the
group who records everything — and no idea what to do with it. He has never thought of
himself as someone who publishes anything.

## Pain point, in the viewer's words

- en-US: "I've got the file. There's nowhere to put it where anyone would actually find it."
- zh-CN: "路线我有，但发哪儿都没人看得到。"

## One-sentence viewer benefit (the core conclusion this video proves)

Your GPX becomes a place other riders can find — with your name on it — by sending one file.

## Five-dimension scores (1–5, with one line of justification each)

| Dimension | Score | Why |
|-----------|------:|-----|
| User relevance 用户相关 | 4 | Narrower than a mission episode — it only lands for riders who already have a file — but for them it's exact. |

## Topic formula (one line)

Pain (I have a file and nowhere to put it) + solution (he built the map, and the rider who
sent the first GPX is credited on it) + benefit (send yours and it goes on the map too).

The structure is inverted against the field episodes: the finished thing is stated in the
first line and the rest is how it got there.

## Designed reactions (the judgment test — all four must have real answers)

- Like because: someone's file actually got used, and he's named for it.
- Save because: **genuinely strong here**, unlike E001–E003 — a viewer with a GPX saves this
  to come back when they're at a computer. First episode in S03 where "save" isn't forced.
- Comment because: the CTA invites the comment directly. **ASSUMPTION:** the reply volume
  is worth more than the debate the field episodes generate; unproven.
- Follow because: it shows the map is being built continuously, not just counted — the
  100-track challenge feeds the same surface a viewer can contribute to.

## Go / no-go question for the human

1. **The uploader credit is missing from the zh-CN read.** The en-US says "with him as the
   uploader"; the Chinese take has no equivalent clause. Crediting the rider is the point
   of the episode, so the variants currently make different promises. Re-record the line,
   carry it in the zh subtitle/overlay, or accept the difference — but it should be a
   decision, not an oversight.
2. Does the app actually show an uploader credit on a trail today? Same trap as E002's
   e-bike commitment and E003's "let's add it to the map" — this one names a specific UI
   affordance, so it's the easiest to falsify by a viewer who goes and looks.
3. Both reads run ~20–21s against the 19s the scripts were written to. Fine for the
   platforms; flagged because the beat targets are the shooting plan.
