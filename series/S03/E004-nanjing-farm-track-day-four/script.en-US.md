## hook (≤2s)

**VO:** I showed up to the best farm I wish I had.

**Caption focus:** "**the best farm I wish I had**" — 2.28s. The line is a joke and a thesis at
once; the picture should be the farm, wide, immediately.
**Unlike zh-CN, the music is already playing under this beat.** The bed starts with the
picture — no lag (see edit-notes.md), so there is no dry hook here to protect.
