## Viewer (one concrete person)

A Chinese rider two seasons in. Can clear a jump, lands it, and loses the corner after it
every single time — and has never been told why, because nobody at their level knows and
nobody above it thinks to mention it. They watch track videos for places to go and have
stopped expecting to learn anything from them.

## Pain point, in the viewer's words

"跳是能跳过去了，但落地之后就是接不上。" They can name the symptom and not the cause. Every
piece of advice they've had is about the air — posture, balance, throttle — and the actual
problem is that they arrive on the ground in the wrong gear with no time left to fix it.

## One-sentence viewer benefit (the core conclusion this video proves)

The half second between you and a good rider isn't in the air, it's that they already
finished the landing before they got there — and the concrete version of that is: **shift
down while you're still in the air, so you land in the gear the corner needs.**

## Topic formula (one line)

Location (最野的农家乐) + expectation (神仙日子) + reversal (学到了一个新手不可能知道的知识)
+ payload (空中降一档) + counter (第四条赛道).

The structure is bait-and-switch, and it works: the first 20 seconds sell a place, and then
the episode turns out to be about a skill. The Li Bai quote at beat 2 and its callback at
beat 15 bracket the whole thing, so the turn doesn't read as two videos stapled together.
