**Spoken:** Our **barber** here runs a local dirt bike track on weekends.

**On-screen / subtitle emphasis:** "**barber**" — the payoff to the haircut turn, and the episode's actual premise: the track and the man cutting your hair are the same lead. Take 2 lands this in one line where take 1 took two.

## hook-a-brother-up

**Spoken:**
- **Founder:** Hook a brother up.
- **Barber:** Say less.

**On-screen / subtitle emphasis:** the comedy beat, and the shortest in the episode — **1.97s** for the whole exchange. Cut it fast.

**The barber's line is his own voice**, not the founder reading it (VOX_001, 18.54–19.43s). The founder's original read of it — "Alright, bet — say less." — is gone from the master. Subtitle it as a **second speaker**: it needs a name tag, a colour, or a position change, or the audience will hear one man answering himself. The bed ducks **10 dB** across the whole exchange (17.60–19.55s) — his line and yours — so it plays as a conversation.

**On-screen / subtitle emphasis:** "**10 years**" — this is the credibility beat; the track exists because one person kept it alive, which is the argument the whole S03 mission rests on.
