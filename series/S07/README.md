# S07 — Riding technique

How to ride a dirt bike, one skill per episode, taught to someone who has only ever ridden an
e-scooter. Each episode is a short numbered drill — 第一步, 第二步, 第三步 — with a coach's English
aside between steps, cut to a rock bed, and it closes by naming the next episode's skill. It began
as S06E002 and moved here: S06 explains a mechanism with diagrams, this season teaches a body.

## Episodes

| ID | Slug | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| E001 | `riding-stance` | Three habits that turn an e-scooter rider into a dirt-bike rider: the balls of the feet on the pegs, two fingers on the levers with elbows up, hips back and chin over the bars. zh-CN, over Highway To Hell. | `packaging` |
